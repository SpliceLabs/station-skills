---
name: ask-for-access
title: Ask for access to…
description: Ask a Station administrator for the use of a docking or a vendor connection's tools.
for: everyone
tools: station_find station_request_access
arguments:
  what: required. The docking, service or vendor tools you want to use
  reason: Why you need it, in a few words
---
Help me ask for access to what I name below.

If I named it loosely, call station_find to work out which docking I mean, and check with me before going on. Then ask me for the reason if I gave none, show me exactly what will be requested and why, and only once I agree call station_request_access. If you are an agent acting for me, say whether it is for me (`for: "owner"`) or for you (`for: "agent"`, only for a vendor connection's tools). Tell me that a Station administrator other than me decides it, and that nothing changes until they do.

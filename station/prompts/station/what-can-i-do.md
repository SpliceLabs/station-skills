Tell me what I can do in this Station.

Call station_whoami and tell me, in plain words, who I am signed in as, the roles I hold, and the dockings and factories I may use, with the tools each one gives me. If station_whoami says something is waiting for me, say how many of each and offer to show them. If I may use nothing yet, say what station_whoami suggests as my next step.

If I ask what this Station is or who runs it, call station_about. Do not call any tool that changes anything.
